SPTuned

Vanilla drop rates assume thousands of players trading online. SPTuned retunes them for one person playing offline, without Magic Find. Each part below is switched on or off in settings.toml; the build always starts from vanilla data.

Runes

preset sp

Each rune is a fixed step rarer than the one below it, and runes take a bigger slice of gem/jewelry/rune drops. No rune drops less often than vanilla from any source.

Bosses

on

Duriel, Mephisto, Diablo and Baal drop as on the first (quest) kill every time: more equipment picks, better quality odds, no gold or junk. Hell bosses keep their vanilla Ancient Statue chance.

Details in the README.

Mercs

0.2

Reviving a mercenary costs one fifth of vanilla, capped at 10,000 gold instead of 50,000.

Details in the README.

Build

Extract the vanilla tables from your own install once, edit settings.toml, then build. The mod goes to <game_dir>/mods/SPTuned.

python tools/casc_extract.py --game "H:/Diablo II Resurrected" \
  --dll path/to/CascLib.dll --filter "excel/" --out reference
python build.py            # --dry-run to preview

Launch

Battle.net launcher → Diablo II: Resurrected → Game Settings → Additional command line arguments. Mods run offline only, so online characters are never affected.

-mod SPTuned -txt

Numbers assume Hell level-85 farming at 900 normal, 80 champion and 20 unique kills an hour, no Magic Find, /players 1. Pages are regenerated by tools/make_page.py, make_loot_page.py, make_uniques_page.py and site_nav.py.
