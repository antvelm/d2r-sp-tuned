Single-Player Rune Ladder

In vanilla Hell, monsters drop runes so rarely that a solo player sees about one Ber per thousand hours. This mod keeps every other drop as it is and makes high runes reachable in a single-player playthrough. All numbers below are computed from the game's own treasureclassex.txt, extracted from this install.

Which runes a Hell rune drop gives

When an Act 5 Hell monster does drop a rune, this is the chance of each one (log scale). Vanilla drops are almost all low runes. The new curve makes every rune exactly × rarer than the one below it, so high runes become rare instead of nearly impossible.

How it works

1 · Flatten the rune ladder

Each Runes N group holds two runes plus a weighted fall-through to Runes N−1. In vanilla, Runes 17 gives Zod weight 1 against 5,170 for falling through, so almost every roll slides down to the low runes. The mod recalculates every fall-through weight from one curve controlled by q.

2 · More runes in the gem/jewelry/rune roll

Every monster drop table has a small chance to roll the gem/jewelry/rune group (Act N Good). The rune entry in that group is multiplied by F. Each monster table then picks the group more often, so rings, amulets, jewels, charms and gems keep their vanilla rates (±0.7% from rounding). The added weight comes out of "drop nothing", so equipment and gold drops don't change at all. In the modded file, the weights in those monster tables are 10 times larger so rounding stays precise. Only their ratios matter.

What stays the same

  • Equipment and gold rates are exactly the same as vanilla, and gem and jewelry rates are within 0.7%.
  • Magic Find doesn't affect runes in either version, so this mod doesn't interact with it.
  • Countess keeps her own rune table. She gets the new curve too, but her rune share isn't multiplied, because she is already the best low and mid rune source.
  • Terror-zone and Herald drop tables use the same Good groups, so they scale the same way.
  • Cube rune upgrades are untouched. Making upgrades cheaper (2 runes into 1) is an optional extra lever.
  • The mod only loads with -mods, so Battle.net characters are never affected.

Mercenary revive cost (separate small change)

The revive price is set in hireling.txt, not in the game code. Every mercenary row has resurrectcostmultiplier = 15, resurrectcostdivisor = 2 and resurrectcostmax = 50000.

Proposed: divisor 2 → 10 and max 50,000 → 10,000. That makes every revive cost one fifth of vanilla, including the cap.

Auto-revive after 30 seconds is not something these data tables can express. It stays a separate mercenary mod with the alternatives discussed earlier (tougher mercenary, regeneration).

Decisions for you

  1. Preset. SP is the recommendation: about 20 runes an hour, Ist every ~6 h, Ber every ~12 h of farming, and much faster with Baal runs. Any F and q values in between work too.
  2. How the mods combine. D2R loads only one mod at a time. The plan is for every repo (runes, mercenaries, uniques) to patch the same mods/SPTuned folder from your own extracted files, so they stack.
  3. Where the mercenary cost lives. It can go in this repo now or in a separate d2r-sp-mercs repo.

Next: bosses and uniques

The data supports the "first kill every time" idea. Bosses have separate quest-drop tables (Baalq (H), Diabloq (H), Mephistoq (H)) with more picks and better odds, and the regular tables can be pointed at those. Andariel already uses Andarielq (H) every time. Replacing Magic Find with fixed, tuned unique and set rates is a separate mod and a separate proposal.

Method: the expected value of every drop chain, following the game's rules for picks, negative (guaranteed) picks and drop-nothing scaling. It ignores the six-item cap per kill and monster-level upgrades to drop tables. Sources: tools/tc_calc.py and tools/runes_mod.py in this repo.

Blizzard's game files are not in this repo. The scripts read them from your own install.
