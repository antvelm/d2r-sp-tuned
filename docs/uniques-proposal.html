What changes in the game files

New tables. 33 SP Chase uL treasure classes, chained by unique item level 20 → 87. Each lists the base items of the uniques up to that level with the Unique quality modifier at 1024, so a pick is always the unique.

One extra entry in 406 drop tables (402 in excel/base): normal, champion, unique, boss, quest-boss and terror-zone tables in all three difficulties. Each gets the chase table for its monster level. The weight is about 2% of the table's equipment weight, so bosses and champions roll it more often. A Hell Baal kill drops one about 1 time in 9.

Nothing else drops less from normal monsters or bosses. The weight comes out of NoDrop. Champion and unique-monster item tables have no NoDrop, so there it comes out of their equipment entry: about 2% fewer of their regular items. The rune floor check still passes.

Shared bases. Nightwing's Veil (with Veil of Steel), Heaven's Light (with The Redeemer) and Stormlash (with Horizon's Tornado) get a bigger weight, so the listed unique still hits the target and the sibling gets the rest. Templar's Might also rolls Tyrael's Might at its normal 1-in-9 share.

Item level 86–87 (Crown of Ages, Arachnid Mesh, Tomb Reaver, Stormlash) need monster level 86+, so in level-85 areas only champions, unique monsters and bosses roll them, as in vanilla.

Nightmare. Fewer uniques are eligible at lower levels, so each one comes faster. A dash means the unique's item level is above what Act 5 Nightmare monsters roll.

Not changed: uniqueitems.txt, itemratio.txt, weapons and armor tables. Change the list with exclude / extra in the [uniques] section of settings.toml; python tools/uniques.py report recomputes the Hell column.
