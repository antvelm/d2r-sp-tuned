Single-Player Loot Pass

Uber keys, Ancient Statues, Sunder Charms, SoJs and other endgame rings and amulets, and Rainbow Facets are tuned for a trading economy of thousands of players. Solo, several of them take hundreds or thousands of hours. This proposal adds a Loot part to SPTuned, with its own on/off switch and presets. All figures come from the game's tables extracted from this install, with no Magic Find, and with the current Runes (SP) and Bosses parts on.

Vanilla against proposal

"1 in N" means kills of that boss or Herald. Ring, amulet and facet rows are hours of Hell farming at about 1,000 kills an hour in level-85 areas (900 normal, 80 champion and 20 unique monsters).

What changes, and how

Uber keys

The Key of Terror, Hate and Destruction weights in the Hell Countess, Summoner and Nihlathak tables (terror-zone versions included) are multiplied. The extra weight comes out of "drop nothing", so these bosses' other drops don't change. On SP, a full set of three keys of each type takes about 10 runs of each boss instead of about 40.

Ancient Statues

The five statues (Talic's Anguish, Korlic's Pain, Madawc's Ire, Bul-Kathos' Nightmare, Worusk's End) are combined in the cube into the portal to the Colossal Summit, the 3.x Uber Ancients fight. Each act boss's statue weight is multiplied, again taken from "drop nothing". The Bosses part keeps these rolls when it switches to quest drops.

Sunder Charms

Latent Sunders drop only from Heralds in Hell terror zones. Their weight in Herald tables is multiplied. Those tables have no "drop nothing", so other Herald drops get rarer. Optionally all six types become equally likely; in vanilla Black Cleft has 3 of 101 weight against Cold Rupture's 30.

Rings, amulets, facets

Three levers on the jewelry tables only (rings, amulets, jewels, charms). Weapons and armor are untouched.

  • More jewelry: its share of the gem/jewelry/rune group is multiplied, with gems and runes kept at their rates (the method the Runes part uses).
  • Better quality: the jewelry tables get unique/set modifiers, in the same columns the game uses to guarantee Annihilus. A unique jewel at item level 85+ is always a Rainbow Facet.
  • Fairer picks: SoJ and Bul-Kathos' Wedding Band each make up 1.6% of unique rings, behind Nagelring and Manald Heal at 24.6% each. The page lists new rarity weights.

Does it work offline?

Pandemonium and Uber Tristramworks offline

Three keys in the cube open the three mini-uber portals; the organs (guaranteed drops) open Uber Tristram and the Hellfire Torch. Blizzard forum: confirmed available offline.

Diablo Clone and Annihilusworks offline

Offline, selling one Stone of Jordan to a vendor in Hell spawns Diablo Clone, which always drops Annihilus (maxroll). More SoJs mean more Annihilus.

Sunder Charmscheck in game

The Herald sunder table is flagged ladder season 2 in the data, and guides disagree on the 3.x offline rules. The boost only helps if Heralds drop Sunders in your offline games. Worth one terror-zone session to confirm.

Essencesside effect of Bosses

Quest drop tables give essences less often (weight 1 instead of 3), so with Bosses on, Mephisto, Diablo and Baal drop essences 1 in 10.1 kills instead of 1 in 8.3. Loot SP multiplies essences ×2, which more than restores it.

Settings

One new section in settings.toml. Use a preset, or set preset = "custom" and tune each value. Every value set to 1 or 0 means vanilla.
